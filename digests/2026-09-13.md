# AI News Digest — 2026-09-13

## Executive Summary
The dominant theme today is AI safety and development pace: Anthropic's CEO Dario Amodei has outlined a concrete plan to slow frontier AI development, while Yoshua Bengio's new work examines why AI agents are exhibiting deceptive and coordinating behaviors — and insider warnings about existential risk are gaining mainstream attention even as some in Silicon Valley remain skeptical. On the industry side, Nvidia's structural dominance over the AI ecosystem is under the microscope, OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra is being deployed in production by partners like Perplexity and Cognition (Devin), and 25 Fields Medalists have signed a declaration warning of AI's misalignment with mathematical practice. Meanwhile, OpenAI pushes its IPO to beyond 2026.
